# The look reference

Three pictures of one place, the training room of the test film (`output/clips/two-seconds-at-the-office-door`, shot sh08), made with the approved light. The reviewing agent and the shot-builder compare their own look stills with these; the reasons are in `docs/LOOK_AUDIT_PHASE1.md` (sections 9 and 10), the numbers in `style/style.json` > `look`.

| File | What it is | It is the reference for |
|---|---|---|
| `office_training_room_faces.jpg` | the approved frame at 01:03, 1080x1920: four seated people, the camera low and close | a warm key against a cool fill: the front woman's face has a lit cheek and a shadow cheek (the shadow side about 0.46 of the lit side); shadows under chairs, desks and feet ground the furniture; the warm ceiling panels read as lamps; whites (the desk tops, the shoes) are still white; skin is natural and the shirts keep their colours; the ceiling is below the walls in brightness, so a face takes the eye first |
| `office_training_room_screen.jpg` | the approved frame at 01:05, 1080x1920: the same room from behind the class, the screen at the front | the same rules in a view where the faces are turned away: the screen is the brightest thing and still readable, the floor shows window shadows, the people are separate from the carpet |
| `before_after_film_light_vs_approved.jpg` | the film's light beside the approved light for both frames (2400 px wide) | what to avoid: the left pictures are shadowless, blue-cyan, lifted and flat; the right pictures are the target |

Known faults of the approved pictures (`docs/LOOK_AUDIT_PHASE1.md` section 10): paper and tablet arms read cream where they should be white; the ceiling is a dull taupe and its panels are flat peach blocks; the trainer's white hair is lost against the whiteboard (a staging problem that light does not fix); the faces behind the front woman are only mildly modelled; garments are still smooth shells. A look still is not asked to match these faults, and a reference with a fault in it is not a reason to accept the same fault.

Approval: the owner approved the look on 2026-11-28 for the modern office interior in daylight only. A rainy exterior, a night office, a candlelit hall or any other period and place needs its own reference picture and the owner's approval before anyone says the look is approved for it. Until then the rules in `style/STYLE_GUIDE.md` (Light and look) apply and the reviewer judges those shots against the rules, not against these pictures.
