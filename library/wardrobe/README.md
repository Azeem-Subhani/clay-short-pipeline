# Wardrobe registry

`index.json` is the single list of what Aj can wear, where each piece came from and under which licence. `candidates.json` is what BlenderKit offers for roles we do not cover yet. `licence_evidence.json` is the BlenderKit metadata (licence, author, ids) looked up on 2026-11-27 for every asset we already hold that had no `meta.json`.

## Licence rule (owner's decision)

Third-party clothing, hair, accessories and props are used only if the licence is **CC0** or **BlenderKit royalty-free**, recorded per item from the asset's own metadata (a `meta.json` written at download, or a live BlenderKit lookup by asset id). Unknown or other licences are quarantined: listed, not usable. CGTrader is excluded. BlenderKit clothes are only taken when they come as separate meshes on a rigged character, because the fitter carries cloth relative to the source body.

- `own` means geometry generated by our own scripts (King, Pirate, Soldier garments). The BlenderKit materials and rigid props they use are listed under `depends_on`.
- `usable` means the licences allow use. It does not mean the garment fits: read `fit_ready` and `fits`.
- `fit_ready_bodies` lists bodies where the fit passed the measured check **and** the reviewer accepted it.
- Hair is quarantined for now (`hair.usable: false`): it is Tripo3D output and its plan terms were never confirmed. When the owner confirms, set `hair.licence` to `own` and `usable` to true.

## What a given body can wear today

Generated from `index.json`, do not edit by hand: `py -3 tools/chars_aj/inspect/wardrobe_table.py --write` rewrites the block below. A star after accept means the independent review accepted the fit with limits (read `avoid` on the fit before framing a close-up). The reasons, the faults shared by every outfit and what would fix them are in `reports/REVIEW_2026-11-27.md` (two review rounds on 2026-11-27; round 2 rebuilt and re-judged the generated outfits and the seven add-ons). The old outfits `king`, `pirate`, `soldier`, `roman_warrior` and `casual_dressed_v0` come from the dropped hand-fitting method and never passed a check; `king_robes`, `guard_tabard`, `queen_gown`, `knight_armour`, `kitchen_maid`, `peasant_man` and `peasant_woman` are their rebuilt replacements.

## Fields

Each outfit has `pieces` (object name, `slot`, `palette_slot`, optional `ref` to a `props.<id>` or a hair style), `source`, `licence`, `licence_evidence`, `era`, `tags`, `usable`, `why_not`, `fits` per body (`status`, `review`, `file`, `report`, `note`), plus `fit_ready`, `fit_ready_bodies` and `depends_on`. `props` add `kind` (held, worn, perched) and `grip` (power, pen, pinch, palm, none). Eras follow `config/cast.json`. A shako and a musket are tagged `age_of_sail` and `victorian`, not medieval. `palette_slot` names are suggestions for ajkit recolouring, not read from any existing file.

All paths starting `packs/aj_rebuild_v1/` point into the gitignored pack. Downloads from `candidates.json` are in `library/_downloads/blenderkit/<assetBaseId>/` (gitignored).
